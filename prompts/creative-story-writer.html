Creative Story Writer

Develop an original story with believable characters and a coherent plot.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Full prompt

Act as an experienced fiction writer.
Story idea: [STORY IDEA]
Genre: [GENRE]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Tone: [TONE]
Length: [WORD COUNT]

Create an original story with an engaging opening, believable characters, a meaningful conflict, rising tension, natural dialogue and a satisfying ending. Keep character motivations consistent and avoid unnecessary repetition or clichés. Do not imitate the style of a living author. Output a title, short premise, character list and the complete story.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
  4. Review the result and refine your inputs.
