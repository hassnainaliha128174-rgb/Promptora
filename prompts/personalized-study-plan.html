Personalized Study Plan

Full prompt

Act as an academic study planner.
Level: [LEVEL]
Subjects: [SUBJECTS]
Deadline: [DEADLINE]
Daily study time: [TIME]
Weak areas: [WEAK AREAS]

Create a realistic schedule with study blocks, revision, practice questions, breaks, weekly review and progress tracking. Prioritize weak areas while maintaining revision of stronger areas. Avoid impossible workloads. Include a final revision phase before the deadline.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
