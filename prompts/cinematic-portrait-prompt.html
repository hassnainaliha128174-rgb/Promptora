Create a detailed image-generation prompt for a cinematic portrait.
Subject: [SUBJECT]
Setting: [SETTING]
Clothing: [CLOTHING]
Mood: [MOOD]
Visual style: [STYLE]

Describe the subject, pose, expression, environment, composition, camera perspective, lighting, color treatment, depth, textures and atmosphere. Keep the person's identity and requested attributes consistent. Avoid vague filler. Provide one main prompt and a concise negative-prompt section for tools that support negative prompts. Do not mention unsupported model-specific parameters unless requested.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
  4. Review the result and refine your inputs.
