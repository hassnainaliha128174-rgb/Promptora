30-Day Content Calendar

Build a realistic social content calendar.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Full prompt

Act as a social-media content strategist.
Brand: [BRAND]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Platforms: [PLATFORMS]
Goal: [GOAL]
Posting capacity: [CAPACITY]

Create a 30-day content calendar. Include date/day, platform, content pillar, topic, format, hook, CTA and a short production note. Balance educational, entertaining, trust-building and promotional content. Keep the plan realistic for the stated capacity and avoid repeating the same concept.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
  4. Review the result and refine your inputs.
