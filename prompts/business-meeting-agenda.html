Business Meeting Agenda

Create a focused agenda for a business meeting.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Full prompt

Act as an experienced meeting facilitator.
Purpose: [PURPOSE]
Participants: [PARTICIPANTS]
Duration: [DURATION]
Decisions needed: [DECISIONS]

Create a focused meeting agenda with time allocations, discussion questions, decision points, required preparation and expected outcomes. Keep the agenda realistic for the available time and avoid topics that do not support the meeting purpose.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
  4. Review the result and refine your inputs.
