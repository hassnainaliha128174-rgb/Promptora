Competitor Research Framework

Create a framework for researching competitors without fabricated data.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Full prompt

Act as a market-research strategist.
Business: [BUSINESS]
Market: [MARKET]
Known competitors: [COMPETITORS]

Create a competitor-research framework covering positioning, products/services, pricing to be verified, audience, distribution, content, customer experience and differentiators. Do not invent competitor information. Provide a comparison table template and a list of evidence that should be collected before drawing conclusions.
HOW TO USE

  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
