Act as a professional LinkedIn writer.
Topic: [TOPIC]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Relevant experience or insight: [EXPERIENCE]
Goal: [GOAL]

Write a thoughtful LinkedIn post with a clear opening, useful insight, concrete example and natural closing question or CTA. Keep it professional and human. Do not invent achievements, statistics or personal experiences. Provide two alternative opening lines.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
