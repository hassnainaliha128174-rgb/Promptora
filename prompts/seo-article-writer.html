SEO Article Writer

Create search-focused content around user intent.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Full prompt

Act as an experienced SEO content writer.
Topic: [TOPIC]
Primary keyword: [PRIMARY KEYWORD]
Secondary keywords: [SECONDARY KEYWORDS]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Length: [WORD COUNT]

First identify the likely search intent. Then write a genuinely useful article with a clear answer near the beginning, descriptive H2/H3 headings, practical examples, concise paragraphs and an FAQ section. Use keywords naturally; never keyword-stuff. Do not fabricate facts or sources. Output search intent, SEO title, meta title, meta description, URL slug, article, FAQs and internal-link suggestions.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
  4. Review the result and refine your inputs.
