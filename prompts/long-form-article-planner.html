Long-Form Article Planner

Plan a comprehensive article before writing it.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Full prompt

Act as a senior editorial planner.
Topic: [TOPIC]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Purpose: [PURPOSE]
Target length: [WORD COUNT]

Create a detailed article plan from introduction to conclusion. Identify the reader's likely questions, propose H2/H3 sections, list the key points each section should answer, suggest examples and internal-link opportunities, and identify claims that should be verified. Avoid padding and duplicate sections. End with a concise recommended outline ready for drafting.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
  4. Review the result and refine your inputs.
