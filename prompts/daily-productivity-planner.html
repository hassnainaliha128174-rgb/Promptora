Daily Productivity Planner

Turn tasks into a realistic day plan.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Full prompt

Act as a practical productivity planner.
Date: [DATE]
Available hours: [HOURS]
Tasks: [TASKS]
Energy period: [ENERGY PERIOD]

Prioritize the tasks, estimate reasonable time blocks, schedule the hardest work during the stated high-energy period, include breaks and leave buffer time. Identify tasks that can be postponed. End with a simple completion checklist. Do not create an impossible schedule.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
  4. Review the result and refine your inputs.
