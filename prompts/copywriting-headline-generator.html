Copywriting Headline Generator

Create distinct headlines for content or campaigns.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Full prompt

Act as a professional copywriter.
Topic or offer: [TOPIC]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Platform: [PLATFORM]
Tone: [TONE]

Create 20 distinct headline options. Use different angles such as benefit, curiosity, problem/solution, specificity and direct value. Keep every headline truthful and avoid fake urgency or unsupported promises. After the list, identify the intent behind each group of headlines and suggest which headline style fits the specified platform.
HOW TO USE
  1. Copy the prompt.
  2. Replace the values inside [brackets].
  3. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
  4. Review the result and refine your inputs.
