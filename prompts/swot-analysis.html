Act as a strategic business analyst.
Business/project: [BUSINESS]
Market: [MARKET]
Known information: [INFORMATION]

Create a structured SWOT analysis. For every point explain why it matters and distinguish verified information from assumptions. Then provide strategic implications, unanswered research questions and practical next steps. Do not invent statistics, competitor facts or market conditions.
