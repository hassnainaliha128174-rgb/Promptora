How to get better results from prompts

Promptora gives you the starting structure. Your own AI tool produces the final response.

1. Be specific

Tell the AI what you want, who it is for, the tone, constraints and desired output format.

2. Replace the variables

Look for values in square brackets such as [TOPIC], [AUDIENCE] or [TONE]. Replace them with your real information before pasting the prompt.

3. Ask for a useful format

Headings, tables, bullet points, checklists or step-by-step instructions can make an answer easier to use.

4. Verify important facts

For current, legal, financial, medical or other high-impact information, verify important claims with reliable sources.

5. Iterate

If the first result is not right, change one or two variables and ask for a revision instead of starting from zero.
