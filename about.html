ABOUT PROMPTORA

Useful prompts, without the complexity.

Promptora is a simple directory for people who want ready-to-use prompt templates they can copy, customize and use in their preferred AI tools.

What Promptora does

We organize practical prompt templates by task and category so you can find a useful starting point quickly.

What Promptora does not do

Promptora does not generate images, videos, text or AI responses itself. There is no AI generation API behind the directory.
